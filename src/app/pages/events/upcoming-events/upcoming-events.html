Edu Carnival Upcoming Education Exhibition Series

Edu Carnival is set to launch its upcoming education exhibition series, beginning in October in Imphal, followed by November editions in Guwahati, Shillong, Itanagar, and Agartala, and concluding with a major event in January in Bangalore.

These locations have been carefully selected based on strong student demand and market potential, ensuring a highly targeted and well-qualified audience. Each event is expected to attract a substantial number of genuine students and parents who are actively exploring higher education opportunities.

The exhibitions will primarily cater to:

Students who have completed Class 12 and are seeking undergraduate admissions.

Graduates looking to pursue postgraduate or professional programmes.

Students interested in pursuing higher education in India as well as abroad.

Edu Carnival offers participating institutions an excellent opportunity to connect directly with motivated prospective students and their parents in a focused admissions environment.

Limited Stall Availability

To maintain exclusivity and maximise visibility for each participating institution, only 30 to 45 exhibition stalls will be available at each venue. Institutions are encouraged to confirm their participation early, as space is limited.
